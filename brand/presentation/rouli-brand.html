What we heard

The brief

Rouli is a shared e-bike and e-scooter rental service in Monastir. Reserve free online, pick up at a local agency, pay on site. The identity should feel electric, light, local, simple and trustworthy — closer to a calm local service than a generic mobility app.

electriclightlocalsimpletrustworthy

Success: distinct from generic mobility marks, recognisable at 16 px, works in one colour, and feels calm, local and low-friction.

Final design

Grounded

A wheel grounded by its flat contact patch — mobility that stays local.

  • The flat patch turns a familiar wheel into a place: Monastir, not a generic app.
  • The hub repeats in the o, so the symbol and wordmark read as one system.
  • Built on one outer radius, one inner radius and one hub; the small cut keeps the idea at 16 px.
  • Electric green is held for dark surfaces, where it has contrast and charge.

Next steps

Notes

Grounded — the most coherent at header and app-icon sizes, and the clearest fit for a calm, local rental service. The flat contact patch and the hub are the ownable details.

  • Use the compact lockup in the app; the horizontal in wide spaces.
  • Keep the flat contact patch — never crop it.
  • Use the small cut below 24 px.
  • Run a trademark search before launch.
